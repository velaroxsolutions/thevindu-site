## The problem

Most dating apps make appearance the first and fastest filter. Everything else about a person
gets looked at only after a two-second judgement.

I wanted to see what happens if you flip the order.

## What I built

You answer questions across five dimensions: values, conflict, love language, empathy, and
ambition. An attachment-style multiplier adjusts how those scores combine.

You're introduced to people you're compatible with, and as the conversation stays healthy, each
profile opens up one layer at a time, across five layers. It's live on the App Store, and in
closed testing on Google Play.
