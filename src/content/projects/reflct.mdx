## The problem

Most journaling tools treat every entry as a blank page. You can write for a year and the
tool knows nothing more about you than it did on day one.

## What I’m building

Reflct carries context between entries. When you write about a hard week, what comes back
can reference the thing you wrote about three weeks ago, because it’s still holding it.
